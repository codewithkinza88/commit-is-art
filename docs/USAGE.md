# How to use Habit Garden 🌸

A cute daily routine for your habits — morning, day, and night.

## Morning ☀️ — plant and plan

1. Open the **Today** tab.
2. Tap **Plant a new habit** to add one of your own: a name, a category
   (Study, Books, Health, Mind, Daily Life), an optional daily target
   in minutes, and an emoji + pastel colour you love.
3. Changed your mind? Tap a habit's edit button — rename it, change
   the category, target, icon or colour, or delete it.

## During the day ✅ — tick and log

- When you finish a habit, **tick it off** for today. Unticking undoes it.
- For study and reading habits, log your time too: tap **+15 min** or
  enter minutes. Reaching your daily target counts as done, even
  without a tick.
- Example: *Study — DSA / course* (60 min) + 45 minutes logged =
  45 minutes towards your goal, and one more push makes it done.

## Night 🌙 — Tonight's review

Before sleep, open the **Tonight** tab and see how much you achieved
today:

- Habits done out of total, and your % achieved
- Total minutes studied / read, and minutes per habit
- Your streaks, and your best habit of the day
- A simple last-7-days view, one % per day

Be kind to yourself: a small % today is still a seed planted. 🌱

## What each garden stage means 🌷

Open the **Garden** tab to see your plants:

| Stage | Emoji | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seed | 🌱 | Not done yet — waiting for its first day of care |
| Sprout | 🌿 | Done today, or a 1-day streak |
| Growing | 🪴 | 2–3 days in a row |
| Blooming | 🌸 | 4–6 days in a row |
| Full bloom | 🌷 | 7+ days in a row — a whole week strong! |

Every tick waters a plant. Keep the streak alive and your garden
will bloom. 💧
