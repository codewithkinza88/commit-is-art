# How to use Habit Garden 🌸

A cute daily routine for your habits — morning, day, and night.

## Morning ☀️ — plant and plan

1. Open the **Today** tab.
2. Tap **Plant a new habit** to add one of your own: a name, a category
   (Study, Books, Health, Mind, Daily Life), an optional daily target
   in minutes, and an emoji + pastel colour you love.
3. Changed your mind? Tap a habit's edit button — rename it, change
   the category, target, icon or colour, or delete it. Deleting asks
   first: tap Delete, then confirm in the dialog — a habit is only
   removed when you tap Delete a second time.

## During the day ✅ — tick and log

- When you finish a habit, **tick it off** for today. Unticking undoes it.
  A ticked habit turns soft pink, and its card shows how many notes it
  has, your latest note, and when you last did it. Each card also has
  a little coloured chip for its category.
- The Today header shows your total minutes for the day
  ("⏱ N min today") and a morning message with how many habits are
  already done. The Garden tab counts plants by growth stage, and
  Tonight also shows your average best streak.
- For study and reading habits, log your time too: tap **+15 min** or
  enter minutes. Reaching your daily target counts as done, even
  without a tick.
- Example: *Study — DSA / course* (60 min) + 45 minutes logged =
  45 minutes towards your goal, and one more push makes it done.

## Night 🌙 — Tonight's review

Before sleep, open the **Tonight** tab and see how much you achieved
today:

- Habits done out of total, and your % achieved
- Total minutes studied / read, and minutes per habit
- Your streaks, your longest streak across all habits, and your best
  habit of the day
- A streak leaderboard (longest current streak first), the total
  flowers blooming in your garden, and a cheer for your weekly goal
- Your 7-day consistency score, your most consistent habit, and a
  warning if a weekly goal still has minutes left
- A simple last-7-days view, one % per day

Be kind to yourself: a small % today is still a seed planted. 🌱

## What each garden stage means 🌷

Open the **Garden** tab to see your plants:

| Stage | Emoji | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seed | 🌱 | Not done yet — waiting for its first day of care |
| Sprout | 🌿 | Done today, or a 1-day streak |
| Growing | 🪴 | 2–3 days in a row |
| Blooming | 🌸 | 4–6 days in a row |
| Full bloom | 🌷 | 7+ days in a row — a whole week strong! |

Every tick waters a plant. Keep the streak alive and your garden
will bloom. 💧

The Garden tab also shows a count line, e.g. "🌱 2 seeds • 🌿 1
sprout • 🌸 1 blooming", so you can see at a glance how many plants
sit at each stage. It also tells you the next habit to water, and
how much of the garden is watered today ("🌸 N% watered today").

A habit you are taking a break from can be archived instead of
deleted — it hides from the garden but its history stays safe.

Tonight also shows the median best streak, total minutes for the
week, and an evening wind-down message, and notes can be exported
as CSV alongside habits and logs.

## The premium look ✨ (version 1.5.0)

Everything is softer and prettier now: gradient headers, rounded
soft-shadow cards, gold streak pills (a 7-day streak turns gold 🏆),
slim progress bars toward each daily target, and a growth bar under
every plant showing how close it is to its next stage.

## Flower garden 🌷 (version 1.6.0)

The Garden opens with a full flower bed — one flower per streak day,
so the more you keep at it, the more flowers bloom. Every plant has
its own flower, and each stage has its own soft colour (no more
all-green garden). Add and edit work from the labelled buttons on
each habit card (＋15 min, ⏱ Minutes, ✏️ Edit), and your notes are
saved together with everything else.

## Notifications & in-app updates 🔔 (version 1.7.0)

A bell in the header shows how many things need your attention —
habits to water, streaks about to break, weekly-goal warnings, the
evening Tonight review, and new badges. Tap the bell to see them all
inside the app. The app also refreshes itself every minute and rolls
over to the new day at midnight on its own. When a newer version is
out, a pink card appears on Today — tap "Update now ⬇️" and the new
APK downloads inside the app, then tap Install (Android's own
installer popup, one tap, no browser).

## Backup & restore 💾 (version 1.8.0)

In the Garden view, "💾 Backup save karo" saves all your habits,
logs and notes to a dated backup file (in Downloads when it exists).
If the app ever has to be reinstalled, tap "📥 Restore karo" and
pick that file to bring everything back.

## Little extras 🌱 (version 1.9.0)

Every habit card has a "📋 Copy" button for an identical copy, an
effort pill (🟢 Easy / 🟡 Medium / 🔴 Hard), and "planted N days ago".
The Garden spotlights a "🌟 Plant of the day" and shows a Legend
mapping each category to its colour. Tonight previews tomorrow
("Kal: ..."), shows your longest gap between waterings, and names
your busiest weekday. If a week passes with no backup, a gentle
"💾 Backup your garden" notification appears.

## Averages, yesterday and more 🌱 (version 1.10.0)

Tonight shows your average best streak ever and yesterday's minutes,
and welcomes you back warmly if you have been away for 2 or more
days. The Garden names your longest-streak plant, and streaks can be
exported as CSV (habit_id, best_streak, current_streak).
