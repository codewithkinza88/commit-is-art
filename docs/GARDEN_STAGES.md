# Garden Stages 🌱🌷

Every habit in Habit Garden is a plant. Its stage grows with its
streak — the number of days in a row you completed it.

| Streak | Stage | Emoji | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 days | Seed | 🌱 | A tiny seed, waiting for its first day of care. |
| 1 day | Sprout | 🌿 | A little sprout appeared! |
| 2–3 days | Growing | 🪴 | Growing nicely — two days and counting! |
| 4–6 days | Blooming | 🌸 | Your plant is blooming, keep watering it! |
| 7+ days | Full bloom | 🌷 | A beautiful flower — a whole week strong! |

## A tick today counts too

If you tick a habit today, it shows as a sprout straight away, even
if it has no streak yet — today's care already makes it grow.

## The garden message

- No habits yet: "Your garden is empty — plant your first habit and watch it grow! 🌱"
- All plants blooming: "Your whole garden is blooming! 🌸✨ So proud of you!"
- Some plants blooming: "N of your plants are blooming! 🌸"
- None blooming yet: "Your garden is growing — tick a habit to water it! 💧🌱"

## How to keep a plant alive

Tick the habit every day, or log enough minutes to reach its daily
target — reaching the target counts as done, just like a tick.
Miss a day and the streak goes back to zero, but the plant does not
die: tick it again tomorrow and it starts sprouting again.
