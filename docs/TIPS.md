# Habit Garden tips 🌸

Small ideas that make the garden grow — for study, reading and health.

## Study habits

- **Start tiny.** 10 minutes of DSA counts — tick it, then keep going if you can.
- **Same time every day.** A habit tied to a time (after breakfast, before
  bed) is easier to remember than one tied to willpower.
- **Log minutes honestly.** Even 15 minutes is progress; the garden grows
  from real minutes, not perfect days.

## Reading habits

- **Keep the book visible.** A book on your pillow is read more than one
  hidden in a bag.
- **One page counts.** On a busy day, one page still keeps the streak alive.

## When you miss a day

- Missing one day is not failing — the streak restarts, the garden stays.
- **Never miss twice.** That is the whole secret: get back the next day.

## Tonight's review

- Before bed, open Tonight and look at what you did — not what you missed.
- Pick **one** habit to focus on tomorrow (the app's insights can help).
- Tonight now shows yesterday next to today — compare the two and see
  whether you did a little more than yesterday. Even +1% counts. 🌱
