# Privacy — your data stays yours 🌸

Habit Garden is a small, local app. There is no account, no login and
no server collecting your habits.

## What is stored

- Your habits, daily ticks, minutes and notes live in one local file
  on your own device (`habit_garden.json`).
- Nothing is sent to the internet by the app itself.

## What is not collected

- No name, email or phone number is asked for or stored.
- No location, contacts or photos are accessed.
- No analytics or advertising trackers are included.

## Backups

- The backup helper (`app/backup.py`) makes a JSON copy of your habits,
  logs and notes. That file is yours — keep it somewhere safe, or
  delete it, as you like.
- Deleting the local data file removes your habits from the device.

## Sharing

- If you share a screenshot or an export with someone, that is your
  choice — the app never shares anything on its own.
